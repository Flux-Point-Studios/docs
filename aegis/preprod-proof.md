## The Barrier sales

Sales 1 to 3 are ADA/USD Barrier positions. Prices are in USD.

| Sale | Coverage | Premium | Strike | Start (UTC) | Expiry (UTC) | Expire possible after (UTC) |
|------|----------|---------|--------|-------------|--------------|-----------------------------|
| 1 | 75 tADA | 3.0825 tADA | 0.13329 | 2026-11-06 20:45:44 | 2026-11-13 20:45:44 | 2026-11-15 20:45:44 |
| 2 | 30 tADA | 2 tADA | 0.13329 | 2026-11-06 20:48:24 | Cancelled | Cancelled |
| 3 | 24 tADA | 19.4736 tADA | 0.156431 | 2026-11-06 21:04:38 | 2026-12-21 21:04:38 | 2026-12-23 21:04:38 |

Sale 1's premium is the sheet's 4.11% for 7 days at 28% depth. Sale 2's is the two legs' 1 tADA
minimum. Sale 3's is the sheet's 81.14% for 45 days at 15.5% depth. These are test sheets chosen to
exercise the path, and they are no guide to market pricing. Sale 3 sits close to the 15% minimum so
that a real ADA fall can pay a Claim through the live reading, with no test print.

The three C-legs in sale 1 and sale 3 each carry a different stake credential, and each offer's
remainder returns to the book under that same credential. All three sales carry the relay's mark at
metadata label 1986. Each sale's fee output is 1.13784 tADA, the ledger's minimum for that output and
above the 1% rule. None of them makes a Cardano Treasury donation, which matches this generation's
rules.

## The Depeg sales

Sales 4 to 8 are USDC/USD Depeg positions with a 0.94 strike and a 7-day term. Prices are in USD.

| Sale | Coverage | Legs | Premium | Start (UTC) | Expiry (UTC) | Expire possible after (UTC) |
|------|----------|------|---------|-------------|--------------|-----------------------------|
| 4 | 60 tADA | 30 + 30 | 2 tADA | 2026-11-06 22:46:05 | 2026-11-13 22:46:05 | 2026-11-15 22:46:05 |
| 5 | 30 tADA | 30 | 2 tADA | 2026-11-07 04:12:05 | Cancelled | Cancelled |
| 6 | 30 tADA | 15 + 15 | 2 tADA | 2026-11-07 05:20:10 | 2026-11-14 05:20:10 | 2026-11-16 05:20:10 |
| 7 | 30 tADA | 18.214286 + 11.785714 | 2.214286 tADA | 2026-11-07 14:44:03 | 2026-11-14 14:44:03 | 2026-11-16 14:44:03 |
| 8 | 30 tADA | 10.714285 + 19.285715 | 2.285715 tADA | 2026-11-07 21:38:08 | 2026-11-14 21:38:08 | 2026-11-16 21:38:08 |

Each premium is the minimum. A sale pays at least 2 tADA, and each leg pays at least 1 tADA and its
pro-rata share of that 2 tADA, so sales 7 and 8 come to a little over 2 tADA. The sheet's 1.18% rate
comes to less at these sizes. The 0.94 strike sat about 6% under the reading, inside the
sheet's 5% to 50% depth band. Sale 5's buyer cancelled it 12 minutes after the start.

Sales 4, 6, 7 and 8 each fill a resting SaturnSwap V3 order and sell the cover in the same
transaction. The buyer receives the order's tUSDC and the order's owner receives its ADA, while the
position script checks the cover sale as it checks any other. Sales 7 and 8 were signed by a buyer
in a mobile wallet, sale 7 in the Aegis web app and sale 8 through SaturnSwap's interface. Each of
those two transactions is under 3 KB, with a fee near 1.13 tADA.

Sales 5 to 8 carry the relay's mark at metadata label 1986. Sale 4 carries none, so the relay did
not present its body. The validators do not read the mark, and the sale is valid without it. Each
fee output is 1.13784 tADA, as on sales 1 to 3.
